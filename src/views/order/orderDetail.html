policy Return policy

⏰ Return window

Within 7 days of receiving the order.

✅ Eligible for return

  • Product defective from manufacturer
  • Product expired before delivery date
  • Wrong product or quantity delivered
  • Severely damaged, torn, or dented packaging
  • Product does not match website description

❌ Not eligible for return

  • Product seal opened or used
  • More than 7 days after delivery
  • Product damaged due to improper storage by customer
  • Return due to dislike or change of mind

📋 Return process

  1. Contact support via chat or hotline 038.339.2903
  2. Provide order number and photos/videos of defective product
  3. Support confirms and guides return shipment
  4. Refund/exchange within 3-5 business days

💰 Refund method

  • COD payment: Refund via bank transfer
  • VNPAY payment: Refund to original account
  • Return shipping paid by Gray Pet Shop (if fault is ours)
